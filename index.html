Lionheart Group stands as the most dominant corporate entity in the known world of Asterra. Founded by the immortal High Elf lord Aetherion Lionheart, the group has grown from a single trading house into a sprawling empire encompassing technology, military, entertainment, biotech, finance, and beyond.

The group operates through a Holding Company with a full C-Suite executive team, and four noble bloodlines each overseeing key subsidiary pillars of the empire. Lord Aetherion leads as CEO, commanding technology and operations from the apex. Duke Baelzhar serves as Chief Strategy Officer. Lady Elyndra leads as Chief Human Capital Officer. Lady Marianne commands operations as Chief Operating Officer.

Lionheart Group maintains influence in every major kingdom of Asterra. From royal marriages to black market operations, from idol concerts to Tower expeditions — the reach of House Lionheart is truly without limit.

Group Companies

Technology

💻 Lionheart Electronics

Leading manufacturer of magic crystal devices, AI-assisted arcane tools, and portable mana tech for adventurers and corporations alike.

Infrastructure

🏗️ Lionheart Construction

Designs and builds fortress cities, dungeon facilities, magical infrastructure, and industrial zones across Asterra. Led by COO Darius Lionheart.

Finance

🛡️ Lionheart Insurance

Comprehensive coverage for adventurers, artifact owners, guild operations, and Tower expeditions. Largest insurer in the northern kingdoms.

Marine & Air

⚓ Lionheart Shipyard

Constructs the finest sea vessels and airships in Asterra. Supplies both commercial fleets and royal navies.

Life Science

🧬 Lionheart Biotech

Cutting-edge research into monster biology, soul genetics, and regeneration technology. CEO: Velisara Lionheart, the Blood Queen.

Automotive

🚗 Lionheart Motors

Pioneer in mana-powered transportation. From personal carriages to golem-assisted combat vehicles.

Industry

⚙️ Lionheart Heavy Industries

Industrial golem manufacturing, mining operations, and heavy machinery. The backbone of Asterra's construction sector.

Agriculture

🌾 Lionheart Agribusiness

Managed monster farms, mana-infused crops, and potion ingredient cultivation across multiple continents.

Technology

🔮 Lionheart Technologies

AI Rune Systems and Crystal Network infrastructure. CEO: Caelthas Lionheart. First heir candidate and tech visionary.

Finance

🏦 Lionheart Finance

Banking, investment management, and magical asset trading. The largest financial institution outside the royal treasury.

Retail

🏪 Lionheart Retail

Luxury department stores, enchanted goods, and magical accessories. CEO: Sylvaria Lionheart, Golden Empress of Commerce. Now also leads Agribusiness, Foods & Hotels.

Food & Beverage

🍱 Lionheart Foods

Producers of magical energy food, potion-infused cuisine, and premium ration supplies for adventurers and militaries.

Hospitality

Entertainment

🎭 Lionheart Entertainment
